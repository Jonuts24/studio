# **App Name**: ThunderPath Guide

## Core Features:

- Nation Selector: Allow users to choose a specific nation (e.g., USA, Germany, USSR) to view its unique starter grind path.
- Grind Path Visualizer: Display a clear, sequential path of recommended vehicles and research priorities for beginners within the selected nation's tech tree.
- Basic Vehicle Profiles: For each recommended vehicle in the path, provide essential details such as vehicle type, tier, and its role in the starter grind.
- AI-Generated Starter Tips: Leverage a generative AI tool to provide concise, beginner-friendly tips and strategies for playing specific vehicles or navigating particular stages of the grind path, tailored to the chosen nation and vehicle.
- AI Game Mechanic Explainer Tool: Utilize a generative AI tool to simplify and explain core War Thunder game mechanics relevant to new players, such as research points, crew skills, or basic combat maneuvers, as contextual guidance within the guide.

## Style Guidelines:

- The chosen color scheme is dark, reflecting the tactical and focused nature of the game. Primary elements, such as active navigation items or headers, use a muted olive green (`#8A9F60`).
- The main background color is a very dark, desaturated forest green (`#242E1F`), providing a calm and immersive backdrop suitable for reading extensive guide content.
- Accent colors, used for interactive elements and key call-to-actions, are a vibrant, slightly yellow-green (`#C3DD3C`) to provide clear visual contrast and draw attention.
- All text, including headlines and body content, will use 'Inter', a clean and highly readable sans-serif font, chosen for its modern, objective, and neutral aesthetic that complements the informational nature of a guide.
- Icons will be kept minimalist and thematic, using simplified line art to represent different nations (e.g., flag-like shapes), vehicle types (tanks, planes), and progress markers within the grind paths.
- The layout emphasizes clear content hierarchy with a prominent nation selector. Grind paths are presented in an easy-to-follow, potentially linear or accordion-style format, making vehicle details expandable to prevent overwhelming new users.
- Subtle, smooth animations will enhance user interaction, such as fades when switching between nations or expanding vehicle information, ensuring a polished yet non-distracting user experience.
